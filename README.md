## About My Work

My practice connects creative expression, computational thinking, digital literacy, and educational innovation.

I am interested in how learners can move beyond consuming technology to questioning it, understanding it, and using it to create.

I explore the connections between code, art, artificial intelligence, and education through interdisciplinary projects and hands-on experiences.
